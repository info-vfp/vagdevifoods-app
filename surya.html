Cook it right

The same rice, four ways.

Water ratios and timings that actually work for Surya — from a pressure cooker in Nagpur to a rice cooker in a hostel room.

Pressure cooker

The everyday north-Indian default. Works for both packs; black needs one extra whistle.

Pink JSR · Black HMT
01
Rinse twice, not five times

Two changes of water is enough to clear loose starch. Over-rinsing washes off the surface layer that gives Surya its aroma.

02
Soak 20 minutes

Cuts cooking time and helps every grain swell evenly rather than splitting at the ends.

03
Three whistles, medium flame

Add 1¾ cups water per cup of rice. Switch off at the third whistle — the residual pressure finishes the job.

04
Rest before you open

Five minutes with the lid on. Fluff with a fork, never a spoon, so the grains stay whole.

Timings are for one full whistle-free cycle on a medium flame. Adjust by a minute either way for your stove.

Where it comes from

You can point at the mill on a map.

Surya is milled at our own plant on Vijayawada Road, Yadgarpally — not job-worked out, not blended from three traders. Paddy comes in from farmers in the surrounding mandals, gets dried, milled, graded and colour-sorted on the same premises, and leaves as a bag with a lot number on it.

Plant & capability

What the mill is actually made of.

Husk-fired steam, bulk storage and optical sorting — the three things that decide whether rice is consistent.

Steam generation

A 14-tonne boiler, fired on our own husk

The husk taken off the paddy fuels the boiler that par-boils the next batch. Nothing is bought in to run it and nothing is dumped.

Storage

Paddy held in bulk, not in stacks

A warehouse floor built to hold season-scale volume, so we buy when the farmer is selling rather than when the market forces us to.

Quality control

Every stream on one plate

Paddy, brown rice, polished rice, broken and bran — drawn from the same lot and checked side by side before a consignment is cleared.

01 — The location

One site. Paddy fields in every direction.

Miryalaguda mills the paddy of the Nagarjuna Sagar command area — the reason Nalgonda district is called the rice bowl of Telangana. Our plant stands on Survey Nos. 328 to 333, Vijayawada Road, Yadgarpally, inside that belt, so paddy reaches the intake yard the same day it leaves the field.

I

Same-day paddy

Farmers from the surrounding mandals deliver straight to our gate. Nothing sits in a trader’s godown losing moisture and character before it reaches us.

II

One roof, one lot

Drying, milling, grading, colour sorting and packing all happen on the same premises, so a lot number stays intact from weighbridge to warehouse.

III

Dispatch to six states

Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and Telangana — with the Vijayawada highway and the Miryalaguda railhead at the door.

02 — Inside the mill

From the weighbridge to the stitched bag — six stages, all on one premises.

01

Gate sampling

A probe sample is drawn from every load before it is allowed onto the weighbridge.

02

Moisture & grain lab

Moisture, immature grain and foreign matter are read on the spot. Out-of-band loads go back.

03

Drying & storage

Mechanical dryers and steel silos hold paddy at a stable moisture through the season.

04

Milling & polishing

Hulling, whitening and polishing lines, with bran and husk drawn off for by-product sale.

05

Grading & packing

Graded, colour-sorted and filled into 10, 26 and 30 kg packs — or your own printed bag.

06

Loading & dispatch

Trucks and containers load in the yard, with paperwork issued the same day.
